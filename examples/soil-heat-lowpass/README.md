# The ground is a low-pass filter

Three years of hourly air and soil temperature at four depths under Berlin (Open-Meteo, ERA5-Land). Systems idea: the frequency response of a diffusive system: each cycle is damped as e^(−z/d) and delayed by z/d with d = √(2κ/ω). Fourier harmonics in SQL give the daily and annual damping depths, κ, and their ratio against √365; the lag passes 90°, impossible for one RC stage. Caveat: reanalysis soil, thick layers.
