# Paintings

Open-access (CC0) paintings from the Cleveland Museum of Art: an image grid, a table of who made them and when, and their creation dates on one time line. Statistics: a distribution and its quantiles, of dates that are ranges ("c. 1650" is 1645-1655): the median and the middle half are taken over each range's midpoint. Caveat: 24 paintings, one page (skip 120) of about 3,900 in the API's order, not a random sample of the collection; change skip or limit to see how stable the median is.
