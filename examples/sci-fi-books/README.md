# The most-edited science fiction

Open Library's science-fiction subject: the 48 works with the most editions, their covers, when they first appeared and how often they were reprinted, and which decades they come from. Statistics: a rate needs an exposure, and the sample is selected on the outcome. Ranked by editions, old works win because they have had longer to collect reprints; editions per year in print (counted to 2026) reorders the list. A few works carry no usable first-publication year (Open Library records 0); they stay in the table but are left out of the charts. Edition counts include translations and reprints that Open Library merged into one work, so they are a rough measure.
