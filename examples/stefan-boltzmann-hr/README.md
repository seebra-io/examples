# Stefan–Boltzmann on the HR diagram

3,000 random Gaia DR3 stars with good parallaxes: luminosity from brightness and distance, radius from L = 4πR²σT⁴, on an HR diagram with lines of equal radius. At the same temperature, giants are 10× larger and 100× brighter than dwarfs; L ∝ M^4.7 on the main sequence. Checked against Gaia's FLAME and GSP-Phot. Caveats: Malmquist bias, model masses, BC_G valid 3,300–8,000 K.
