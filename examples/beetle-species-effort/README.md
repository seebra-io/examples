# Beetle species and effort

Beetle records in GBIF from three Swedish provinces in 2024, counted per species. Statistics idea: a species count depends on effort: rarefaction gives the species expected in n random records so provinces compare at equal effort, and Chao1 estimates the unseen from species seen once and twice. Caveats: GBIF records are not a random sample, provinces differ in size and habitat, and Chao1 is a lower bound.
