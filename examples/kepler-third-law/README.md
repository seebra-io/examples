# Kepler's third law in 5,600 exoplanets

NASA Exoplanet Archive: period, semi-major axis and star mass of every planet. Physics idea: Kepler's third law P² = a³/M★; dividing by the star's mass collapses all orbits onto one line (slope 0.9985), and the star's mass can be read off any orbit. Forensic twist: about 55% agree to <1% because the archive computed a with the law itself. Caveats: composite rows mix references; planet mass neglected.
