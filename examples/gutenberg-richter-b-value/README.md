# The Gutenberg–Richter law and the b-value

Every M ≥ 4.5 earthquake of 2025 (USGS): the frequency–magnitude distribution, completeness magnitude by maximum curvature, and the b-value by Aki–Utsu maximum likelihood (1.15 ± 0.02; near 1 for Mw alone). Ten times rarer per magnitude unit, yet the M 8.8 Kamchatka quake released about 83% of the year's energy. Caveats: mixed magnitude scales, no declustering.
