# CO₂ and income: elasticity

CO₂ per person against GDP per person (PPP) for every country of a million people or more (World Bank), and six countries' paths since 1990. Statistics idea: an elasticity is a slope on log–log axes: across countries CO₂ rises about 1.1% per 1% of income, but within Sweden, the UK, Germany and the US it fell as they got richer: a slope between groups is not the relation within them, and neither is a cause. Caveats: territorial emissions, latest year per country.
