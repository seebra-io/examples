# The Ramachandran plot: why proteins have helices and sheets

Backbone angles φ and ψ of 606 residues of four proteins (PDBe validation, secondary structure). Half the residues lie in 1.4% of the plane: helices and strands are two sterically allowed basins, positive φ is glycine's, proline's φ is pinned. Caveats: four proteins; approximate region boxes.
